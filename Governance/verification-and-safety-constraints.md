Constitutional Guarantees for Epistemic Integrity, Risk Boundaries, and Canonical Stability in LazarusForgeV0

---

1. Purpose
Verification and Safety Constraints define the non‑overrideable constitutional safeguards that ensure LazarusForgeV0 evolves coherently, safely, and with epistemic integrity. These constraints govern:

- how knowledge becomes trustworthy,  
- how revival outputs become canonical,  
- how risk is assessed and mitigated,  
- how emergent behavior is bounded,  
- how institutional reasoning evaluates correctness.

These constraints supersede all operational modules and sandbox rules.

---

2. Constitutional Mission
This document ensures that LazarusForgeV0:

- adopts only verified, safe, and evidenced knowledge,  
- prevents harmful or unstable architectural evolution,  
- maintains rigorous epistemic standards,  
- enforces human oversight where required,  
- protects the Forge from unsafe emergent behavior,  
- preserves canonical stability.

Verification and safety are constitutional obligations.

---

3. Verification Principles
Verification is the institutional mechanism that separates exploration from specification.

3.1 Evidence Before Adoption
No architectural element may become canonical without:
- sufficient evidence,  
- provenance validation,  
- dependency resolution,  
- maturity assessment.

Evidence is the foundation of verification.

3.2 Verification Gates
Verification gates are mandatory checkpoints that all canonical changes must pass.

Each gate evaluates:
- correctness,  
- evidence sufficiency,  
- dependency stability,  
- safety boundaries,  
- provenance integrity,  
- epistemic maturity.

Verification gates cannot be bypassed.

3.3 Epistemic Maturity
Verification requires classification of maturity:
- Exploratory  
- Provisional  
- Verified  
- Canonical

Only verified elements may be promoted.

3.4 Provenance Validation
Verification must confirm:
- lineage,  
- audit trails,  
- evidence history,  
- reasoning history.

Provenance is part of verification, not an afterthought.

3.5 Dependency Resolution
No element may pass verification if:
- upstream unknowns remain unresolved,  
- downstream dependencies are unstable,  
- cross‑module dependencies are inconsistent.

Verification ensures architectural coherence.

---

4. Safety Principles
Safety is constitutional and cannot be delegated to modules.

4.1 Anti‑Weaponization
The Forge must reject:
- harmful revival paths,  
- dangerous reconstructions,  
- weaponizable outputs,  
- unsafe emergent behavior.

Safety overrides creativity.

4.2 Human Oversight
High‑impact changes require human review.

Oversight is mandatory when:
- safety unknowns exist,  
- emergent behavior is detected,  
- revival identity is ambiguous,  
- architectural risk is high.

Human oversight cannot be automated away.

4.3 Risk Boundaries
Safety requires explicit risk boundaries:
- no harmful outputs,  
- no destabilizing architectural changes,  
- no unverified agent autonomy,  
- no revival paths that violate identity constraints.

Risk boundaries are constitutional constraints.

4.4 Emergent Behavior Control
Emergent behavior is allowed only if:
- it remains safe,  
- it is logged,  
- it does not override constitutional principles,  
- it does not bypass verification gates.

Unsafe emergent behavior must be halted immediately.

4.5 Safety Unknowns
Safety unknowns must be:
- registered,  
- owned,  
- investigated,  
- verified,  
- resolved before adoption.

Safety unknowns block canonical promotion.

---

5. Verification Workflow
Verification follows a constitutional workflow:

1. Evidence Collection  
2. Dependency Mapping  
3. Provenance Validation  
4. Safety Assessment  
5. Verification Gate Evaluation  
6. Human Oversight (if required)  
7. Canonical Promotion

This workflow cannot be altered by modules.

---

6. Safety Workflow
Safety follows a parallel constitutional workflow:

1. Risk Identification  
2. Safety Unknown Registration  
3. Emergent Behavior Analysis  
4. Boundary Enforcement  
5. Human Review  
6. Safety Verification Gate  
7. Approval or Rejection

Safety workflows supersede verification workflows when conflict arises.
