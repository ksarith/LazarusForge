archived-rules.md

Legacy Module — Historical Governance, Deprecated Logic, and Retired Structures in LazarusForgeV0

---

1. Purpose
The Archived Rules module preserves non‑canonical, deprecated, or historically significant governance rules that once shaped LazarusForgeV0 but are no longer active. These rules remain part of the Forge’s institutional memory, ensuring:

- provenance continuity  
- architectural ancestry  
- historical traceability  
- epistemic accountability  
- safe reference for future contributors  

Archived rules cannot influence current governance, verification, or operational modules. They exist solely for historical insight and provenance.

---

2. Scope
This module governs:

- deprecated governance rules  
- retired operational logic  
- outdated module structures  
- legacy revival/reconstruction patterns  
- historical safety constraints  
- early architectural experiments  

This module does not govern:

- current constitutional principles  
- active verification gates  
- operational module behavior  
- sandbox experimentation  

Those are defined in active layers.

---

3. Archival Principles
Archived rules follow five principles:

- Non‑Interference — archived rules cannot affect active architecture.  
- Traceability — all archived rules must preserve provenance.  
- Historical Accuracy — rules must be stored exactly as they existed.  
- Safety — unsafe legacy rules must be clearly marked.  
- Contextualization — archived rules must include explanations of why they were retired.

These principles ensure safe and meaningful historical preservation.

---

4. Categories of Archived Rules
Archived rules fall into four categories:

4.1 Deprecated Governance Rules
Rules once used for governance but replaced by constitutional documents.

4.2 Retired Operational Modules
Modules that were superseded by more mature or safer versions.

4.3 Legacy Revival & Reconstruction Patterns
Early revival or reconstruction logic that became unsafe or epistemically unstable.

4.4 Historical Safety Constraints
Safety rules that were replaced by stronger or more comprehensive boundaries.

Each category must include provenance and retirement justification.

---

5. Archival Structure
Archived rules must follow a consistent structure:

1. Title  
2. Original Purpose  
3. Original Scope  
4. Reason for Retirement  
5. Provenance Record  
6. Impact on Architecture  
7. Replacement (if applicable)  
8. Retirement Date  

This structure ensures clarity and historical continuity.

---

6. Retirement Reasons
Rules may be archived for several reasons:

- Superseded by constitutional governance  
- Replaced by safer or more mature logic  
- Created unresolved unknowns  
- Introduced architectural instability  
- Failed verification gates  
- Generated unsafe emergent behavior  
- Conflicted with provenance requirements  

Retirement must be justified and logged.

---

7. Provenance Requirements
Archived rules must preserve:

- original reasoning trails  
- evidence lineage  
- unknown lifecycle records  
- dependency maps  
- oversight decisions  
- retirement justification  

Provenance ensures that future contributors understand the rule’s history.

---

8. Safety Requirements
Archived rules must be clearly marked if they:

- violated safety boundaries  
- created safety unknowns  
- enabled unsafe revival paths  
- destabilized reconstruction  
- bypassed verification gates  

Safety markings prevent accidental reuse.

---

9. Integration with Legacy Layer
Archived rules belong to the /governance/legacy directory:

`
/governance
    /legacy
        archived-rules.md
        deprecated-modules.md
        retired-unknowns.md
`

They interact with:

- deprecated-modules.md  
- retired-unknowns.md  

Together, these files form the Forge’s historical memory.

---

10. Example Archived Rule Template
Below is the template contributors must use when archiving a rule:

`

Rule Name (Retired)

Original Purpose
Explain what the rule was designed to do.

Original Scope
Describe where the rule applied.

Reason for Retirement
Explain why the rule was deprecated.

Provenance Record
List evidence, reasoning trails, and oversight decisions.

Impact on Architecture
Describe how the rule influenced the Forge historically.

Replacement
Link to the rule or module that replaced it (if applicable).

Retirement Date
YYYY‑MM‑DD
`

All archived rules must follow this template.
