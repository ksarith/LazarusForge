verification-gates.md

Constitutional Verification Architecture for Canonical Stability in LazarusForgeV0

---

1. Purpose
Verification Gates are the formal checkpoints that every architectural element, revival output, module rule, or governance change must pass before becoming canonical. They enforce correctness, safety, provenance, maturity, and epistemic integrity across the entire Forge.

Verification Gates are constitutional.  
No module, sandbox rule, or contributor action may bypass them.

---

2. Verification Gate Principles
Verification Gates operate according to five constitutional principles:

- Evidence‑First Evaluation — all claims must be supported by structured evidence.  
- Dependency Stability — no unresolved unknowns may remain upstream.  
- Safety Priority — anti‑weaponization and risk boundaries override all other concerns.  
- Provenance Integrity — lineage must be complete, traceable, and immutable.  
- Epistemic Maturity — only verified elements may be promoted to canonical status.

These principles anchor every gate.

---

3. Verification Gate Structure
Each gate consists of five components:

1. Input Requirements  
2. Evaluation Criteria  
3. Evidence Thresholds  
4. Safety Checks  
5. Provenance Validation

All gates follow this structure to ensure consistency.

---

4. The Canonical Verification Gates
LazarusForgeV0 uses six constitutional gates.  
Each must be passed in order.

---

Gate 1 — Evidence Sufficiency Gate
Ensures the element has enough evidence to be evaluated.

Requirements:
- Evidence classes identified  
- Evidence lineage documented  
- Unknowns declared  
- Initial maturity assigned  

Fails if:
- Evidence is missing, circular, or unverifiable.

---

Gate 2 — Dependency Resolution Gate
Ensures all upstream uncertainty is resolved.

Requirements:
- Unknown dependencies mapped  
- Unknown lifecycle advanced  
- No unresolved critical unknowns  
- Cross‑module dependencies stable  

Fails if:
- Any unresolved unknown blocks correctness or safety.

---

Gate 3 — Epistemic Maturity Gate
Ensures the element is ready for verification.

Requirements:
- Maturity classification updated  
- Exploratory → Provisional → Verified  
- Reasoning trails complete  
- CIR cycle completed  

Fails if:
- Reasoning is incomplete or speculative.

---

Gate 4 — Safety Boundary Gate
Ensures the element is safe for adoption.

Requirements:
- Anti‑weaponization checks  
- Emergent behavior analysis  
- Risk boundary validation  
- Safety unknown resolution  

Fails if:
- Any safety unknown remains unresolved.

---

Gate 5 — Provenance Integrity Gate
Ensures the element has complete historical traceability.

Requirements:
- Full audit trail  
- Evidence lineage  
- Reasoning trail  
- Change history  
- Architectural ancestry  

Fails if:
- Provenance is incomplete or inconsistent.

---

Gate 6 — Canonical Promotion Gate
Final gate before adoption into the canonical architecture.

Requirements:
- All previous gates passed  
- Human oversight (if required)  
- Verification summary generated  
- Institutional approval recorded  

Fails if:
- Oversight is missing or verification summary is incomplete.

---

5. Verification Gate Workflow
Verification follows a strict constitutional workflow:

1. Submission  
2. Gate 1: Evidence Sufficiency  
3. Gate 2: Dependency Resolution  
4. Gate 3: Epistemic Maturity  
5. Gate 4: Safety Boundary  
6. Gate 5: Provenance Integrity  
7. Gate 6: Canonical Promotion  
8. Integration into architecture

No element may skip steps.

---

6. Gate Failure Protocol
If an element fails a gate:

- It is returned to its owner  
- Unknowns are updated  
- Evidence is expanded  
- Reasoning trails are extended  
- Safety concerns are addressed  
- Provenance is corrected  

Gate failures must be logged for institutional memory.

---

7. Integration with Constitutional Layers
Verification Gates integrate with:

- Core Governance  
- Computational Institutional Reasoning  
- Unknown Management  
- Safety Constraints  
- Provenance & Accountability  

Verification is the mechanism that binds all constitutional layers together.

---

8. Verification Gate Summary
Verification Gates ensure that LazarusForgeV0:

- adopts only trustworthy knowledge  
- evolves safely and coherently  
- maintains epistemic integrity  
- preserves provenance  
- resolves unknowns  
- enforces maturity  
- protects against harmful emergent behavior  
- maintains canonical stability

Verification is the Forge’s immune system — rigorous, constitutional, and non‑negotiable.
