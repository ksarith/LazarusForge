Complete Map of the LazarusForgeV0 Governance Architecture
This is the canonical index for the entire /governance directory. It’s the single page that orients contributors, reviewers, and agents to every governance file, its purpose, and how it fits into the Forge’s epistemic architecture.

---

1. Governance Overview
The governance system of LazarusForgeV0 is organized into five constitutional layers:

1. Constitutional Governance  
2. Verification & Safety  
3. Operational Modules  
4. Sandbox Governance  
5. Legacy Governance

Each layer has a distinct role in maintaining epistemic integrity, architectural stability, and safe evolution.

3. Constitutional Governance
Foundational, immutable documents that define the Forge’s identity.

- Core Governance — constitutional principles and governance hierarchy  
- Computational Institutional Reasoning — epistemic reasoning framework  
- Unknown Management — lifecycle, ownership, and dependency rules  
- Verification & Safety Constraints — constitutional limits on verification and safety  
- Provenance & Accountability — lineage, audit trails, and accountability rules  

These documents cannot be overridden by any module or sandbox rule.

---

4. Verification & Safety
Mechanisms that ensure correctness, maturity, and risk control.

- Verification Gates — six constitutional gates for correctness and maturity  
- Safety Boundaries — anti‑weaponization, emergent behavior limits, and risk controls  
- Maturity Classification — developmental stages: Exploratory → Provisional → Verified → Canonical  
- Oversight Protocols — human‑in‑the‑loop governance for high‑impact changes  

Verification and safety bind all other layers together.

---

5. Operational Modules
Governed, verified, accountable operational logic.

- Agent Interaction — communication, cooperation, and escalation rules  
- Revival Protocols — structured revival of fragments  
- Reconstruction Rules — stabilization and structural maturation  
- Repository Integrity — architectural invariants and consistency enforcement  
- Contributor Workflow — contributor responsibilities and processes  
- Routing & Discovery — fragment discovery and routing logic  

Operational modules implement the Forge’s epistemic machinery.

---

6. Sandbox Governance
Safe, isolated experimentation.

- Experimental Governance — rules for exploration, drafts, and prototypes  
- /sandbox/drafts/ — experimental modules, revival prototypes, speculative governance ideas  

Sandbox outputs are never canonical without verification and oversight.

---

7. Legacy Governance
Historical memory and retired structures.

- Archived Rules — deprecated governance rules  
- Deprecated Modules — retired operational modules  
- Retired Unknowns — unknowns that completed their lifecycle  

Legacy files preserve architectural ancestry and provenance.

---

8. Governance Flow Diagram
A simplified conceptual flow of how governance layers interact:

`
Constitutional → Verification → Operational → Sandbox → Legacy
`

- Constitutional defines what must always be true  
- Verification ensures correctness and safety  
- Operational modules implement logic  
- Sandbox explores new ideas  
- Legacy preserves history
