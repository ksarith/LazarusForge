Operational README

Complete Index of the LazarusForgeV0 Operational Modules
This is the canonical README for the entire /modules directory. It explains how the operational layer works, how modules interact, and how contributors should navigate the system. It’s structured, deep, and aligned with the Forge’s constitutional architecture.

---

1. Purpose of the Operational Layer
The operational layer is where LazarusForgeV0 thinks, acts, revives, reconstructs, routes, and maintains itself. These modules implement the Forge’s epistemic machinery under the constraints of:

- constitutional governance  
- verification gates  
- safety boundaries  
- unknown management  
- provenance rules  

Operational modules are powerful — but they are not sovereign.  
They execute logic; they do not define it.

6. Interaction with Other Layers

Constitutional Layer
Operational modules must obey constitutional rules.  
They cannot override governance.

Verification & Safety Layer
Operational modules prepare content for verification gates.  
Safety boundaries restrict operational behavior.

Sandbox Layer
Experimental modules may be promoted into the operational layer only after verification.

Legacy Layer
Deprecated modules and retired unknowns preserve operational history.
