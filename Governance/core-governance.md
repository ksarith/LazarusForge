
core-governance.md (Constitutional Governance Layer)

LazarusForgeV0 — Constitutional Principles, Verification Guarantees, and Epistemic Foundations

---

1. Purpose
The Constitutional Governance Layer defines the immutable principles that ensure LazarusForgeV0 remains a trustworthy, evidence‑driven, safe, and auditable system for digital revival and architectural exploration. These principles supersede all operational modules, experimental rules, and legacy content.

This document is not operational.  
It establishes what must always be true, regardless of implementation.

---

2. Constitutional Mission
LazarusForgeV0 exists to:

- revive, reconstruct, and reinterpret fragmented digital artifacts,  
- maintain epistemic integrity through verification, evidence, and provenance,  
- manage uncertainty explicitly through unknown tracking and lifecycle rules,  
- ensure safety through anti‑weaponization, human oversight, and verification gates,  
- support structured architectural maturation,  
- enable exploration without compromising canonical stability.

These commitments cannot be overridden.

---

3. Constitutional Principles
These principles define the identity of LazarusForgeV0 and anchor all governance.

3.1 Evidence‑First Architecture
All claims, revivals, reconstructions, and architectural decisions must be supported by:
- evidence classes,  
- verification gates,  
- provenance trails,  
- explicit uncertainty declarations.

Evidence is the foundation of trustworthiness.

3.2 Verification Before Adoption
No specification, rule, or architectural change becomes canonical until it passes:
- verification gates,  
- maturity assessment,  
- provenance review.

Exploration is free; adoption is gated.

3.3 Unknown Lifecycle Management
Unknowns are not documentation — they are architectural dependencies.

The system must:
- register unknowns,  
- assign ownership,  
- track lifecycle,  
- retire resolved unknowns,  
- prevent silent epistemic drift.

Unknown management is constitutional.

3.4 Safety as a Constitutional Constraint
Safety is not a module. It is a constitutional guarantee.

Safety includes:
- anti‑weaponization,  
- human oversight,  
- verification before deployment,  
- prohibition of harmful emergent behavior,  
- strict boundaries for autonomous agents.

Safety cannot be overridden.

3.5 Provenance and Accountability
Every change must preserve:
- provenance,  
- audit history,  
- architectural lineage,  
- contributor accountability.

Institutional memory is a constitutional requirement.

3.6 Separation of Exploration and Specification
Exploration is encouraged.  
Specification is controlled.

The system must:
- isolate experimental behavior,  
- prevent destabilization of canonical architecture,  
- maintain clear boundaries between exploratory and verified content.

---

4. Constitutional Override Hierarchy
This hierarchy defines authority and cannot be changed without constitutional migration.

1. Constitutional Governance (this document)  
2. Verification & Safety Constraints  
3. Operational Modules  
4. Experimental Sandbox  
5. Archived Legacy

Operational modules may reference constitutional principles.  
Sandbox rules may not override any higher layer.

---

5. Verification & Evidence Guarantees
Verification is a constitutional layer, not a module.

5.1 Verification Gates
All canonical changes must pass:
- correctness verification,  
- epistemic maturity checks,  
- provenance validation,  
- safety review.

5.2 Evidence Classes
Evidence must be categorized and traceable:
- empirical evidence,  
- structural evidence,  
- inferential evidence,  
- revival‑derived evidence,  
- uncertainty declarations.

5.3 Epistemic Maturity
Architectural elements must be classified by maturity:
- exploratory,  
- provisional,  
- verified,  
- canonical.

No element may be promoted without passing verification gates.

---

6. Unknown Management Guarantees
Unknowns are first‑class architectural objects.

The system must:
- register unknowns at creation,  
- assign ownership,  
- track dependencies,  
- update maturity,  
- retire resolved unknowns,  
- prevent silent epistemic drift.

Unknowns.md is constitutionally required.

---

7. Safety Guarantees
Safety is constitutional and cannot be delegated.

7.1 Anti‑Weaponization
No revived or reconstructed content may be used to create harmful systems.

7.2 Human Oversight
All high‑impact changes require human review.

7.3 Verification Before Deployment
No agent output becomes canonical without verification.

7.4 Emergent Behavior Boundaries
Emergent behavior is allowed only if:
- it remains safe,  
- it is logged,  
- it does not override constitutional principles.

---

8. Provenance & Accountability Guarantees
The system must maintain:
- full audit trails,  
- change provenance,  
- contributor accountability,  
- architectural lineage.

Provenance is required for trustworthiness.

---

9. Constitutional Migration Rules
Changes to this document require:
- explicit constitutional migration proposals,  
- evidence justification,  
- verification gate passage,  
- provenance logging,  
- human approval.

Operational modules do not require constitutional migration.

---

10. Constitutional Summary
LazarusForgeV0 is governed by:
- evidence,  
- verification,  
- unknown management,  
- safety,  
- provenance,  
- architectural maturity,  
- separation of exploration and specification.

These principles define the identity of the project and cannot be overridden.
